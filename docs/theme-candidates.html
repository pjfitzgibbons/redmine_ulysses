Ulysses theme candidates

Four palettes from Papilio ulysses, the blue swallowtail, and four sets of OFL fonts, for the reference tokens.css (Redmine #94). Pick one font set below and every page mock-up switches to it. Then pick a palette. The final values can still be adjusted after you choose.

Every palette already passes the checks in #94's Done list: every text and ground pair is at least 4.5:1, and white header text on both ends of the gradient is at least 5.6:1, in light and in dark. Each palette's full contrast table is under its mock-ups.

Fonts: sans, mono and serif

Roles follow design 0004 §3.8: ink, muted and faint text; page, surface and sunk grounds; rule; primary and its fill; accent; focus; ok, warn, danger and info, each as ink on soft; and the header gradient with its text.

Fonts here come from Google Fonts for the preview only. The chosen set will be vendored into the plugin as WOFF2 files with its OFL licence, with no font CDN.
